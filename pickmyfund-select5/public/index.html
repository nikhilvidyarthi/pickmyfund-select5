How it works

Sector & metals slots · 40%

Sector fund groups, gold funds and silver funds are ranked together by average 6-month return. The top two go in; a holding stays while it ranks in the top five.

Overseas slot · 20%

Overseas regions (US tech, US broad, Taiwan, China, Japan, Europe, emerging markets and others) are ranked by 6-month return. The slot buys the best fund in the top region that is open for subscription; if none beats an arbitrage fund, it holds arbitrage.

Core slots · 40%

The two steadiest diversified funds across large, flexi, mid and small cap, one per segment, ranked by 1-year return relative to volatility.

Fund choice

Funds above ₹500 cr with a one-year record. A held fund stays while it is in the top three of its group. One fund per fund house.

Review & new money

Signals are checked at every month end. Funds normally change only at the quarterly rebalance (January, April, July, October), when all five reset to 20%. New money waits in an arbitrage fund and moves in over three months.

Returns from to 8 Oct 2026 are reconstructed by applying the model's rules to historical NAVs; they are not returns earned by a client account.
